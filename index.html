About Me

I am an Assistant Professor and Director of Computer Science at Loyola University New Orleans. My work combines computer science education, empirical experimentation, network measurement, and machine learning to investigate how emerging technologies perform in real-world settings.

I am particularly interested in helping students develop strong computational foundations and the ability to use modern tools critically, creatively, and responsibly.

Research Interests

Immersive Multimedia & QoE

Understanding user-perceived quality in 360-degree video and immersive streaming systems.

5G and Network Performance

Analyzing how wireless network conditions relate to multimedia performance and user experience.

Machine Learning for Prediction

Using data-driven methods to predict performance degradation and interpret system behavior.

AI and Responsible Computing

Exploring practical AI applications, evaluation, and responsible use in computing and education.

Selected Publications

International Conference on Modeling, Analysis and Simulation of Wireless and Mobile Systems (MSWiM), 2025.
